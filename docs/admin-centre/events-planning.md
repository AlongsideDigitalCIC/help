title: Events, Services & RSVPs
description: Plan church services, charity fundraisers, community sessions, and track attendee RSVPs.
---

Admin Centre unifies service planning, hall bookings, and attendance registration in one place.

---

## 1. Creating an Event or Service

1. Navigate to **Events** and click **+ Create Event**.
2. Fill in the event details:
   * **Title & Description:** (e.g. *Sunday Morning Worship*, *Community Coffee Morning*, *Youth Group Social*).
   * **Date, Time & Recurrence:** Set as single, weekly, or monthly recurring sessions.
   * **Location / Room:** Track hall bookings to prevent room double-booking.

---

## 2. Managing RSVPs & Capacity

For events with limited seating, catering requirements, or safeguarding caps:
* **Enable RSVP:** Allow members to register themselves and family members.
* **Capacity Limits:** Set a maximum number of attendees with an automatic waitlist.
* **Dietary & Special Requirements:** Collect allergy, accessibility, or dietary needs during registration.

---

## 3. Digital Check-in & Attendance Registers

On the day of the event:
* Coordinators can open the **Check-in Kiosk** on a tablet or phone.
* Quickly search for attendee names or check off families as they arrive.
* Export attendance logs for annual charity reporting or AGM statistics.
