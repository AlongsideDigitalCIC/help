Admin Centre provides a centralised, secure address book designed to keep contact details updated while fully complying with UK Data Protection and GDPR legislation.

---

## 1. Adding Members & Households

People can be added individually or organized into linked **Households**:

* **Adding an Individual:** Click **People &rarr; + New Person** and enter their name, phone, email, and address.
* **Creating a Household:** Link spouses, partners, children, and dependants together under a single household record. This simplifies family mailings and event registrations.

---

## 2. UK GDPR & Consent Tracking

Maintaining explicit consent for church and charity communications is required under UK law. Admin Centre includes built-in consent toggles:

* **General Pastoral / Community News:** Email newsletters, announcements, and prayer chains.
* **Direct Messaging:** SMS / WhatsApp reminders for rotas and events.
* **Photo / Video Consent:** Permission to include photographs in publicity, social media, or service live streams (critical for children and youth).
* **Consent Audit Trail:** Every time a member grants or revokes consent via their portal, the date, time, and method are logged automatically.

---

## 3. Custom Tags & Groups

Organise members into dynamic groups:
* *Small Groups / House Churches*
* *Trustees & Committee Members*
* *Youth & Children*
* *AV / Sound Desk Team*

Tags make it simple to filter rotas, send targeted messages, or generate attendance reports.
