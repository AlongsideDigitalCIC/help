title: Automated Reminders & Notifications
description: Configure email, SMS, and WhatsApp alerts for rotas and events.
---

Reduce no-shows and last-minute scrambles with automated, scheduled reminders sent directly to volunteers' devices.

---

## 1. Notification Channels

Admin Centre supports multiple dispatch channels:
* **Email:** Standard delivery for weekly rotas, event invitations, and monthly schedules.
* **SMS:** High-priority text messages for morning-of reminders and urgent replacement requests.
* **WhatsApp:** Interactive messages where volunteers can reply `YES` to confirm or `SWAP` to request coverage.

---

## 2. Default Rota Reminder Schedule

You can customize the cadence of reminders in **Settings &rarr; Notifications**:

* **5 Days Before:** Initial email reminder with service time and role details.
* **2 Days Before:** WhatsApp / SMS reminder with a quick confirmation link.
* **Morning of Event:** Optional final alert for early-morning setup crews.

---

## 3. Communication Logs & Deliverability

Administrators can view delivery logs under **Settings &rarr; Message History**:
* Track whether an email was *Delivered*, *Opened*, or *Bounced*.
* If a volunteer's email address is invalid, the system flags their profile so a team leader can request an updated address.
