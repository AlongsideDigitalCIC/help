title: Organisation Setup & Tailoring
description: Configure terminology, modules, and team settings for your organisation type.
---

Admin Centre adapts to the terminology and workflows of your organisation. Whether you are an independent church, a regional charity, or a community youth club, the platform moulds to your needs.

---

## 1. Choosing Your Organisation Type

During first-time setup, your administrator selects your primary organisation profile:

* **Church / Faith Community:** Activates Sunday services, worship teams, pastoral care, and parishioner directories.
* **Charity / NGO:** Configures outreach projects, committee meetings, beneficiary logs, and volunteer hours tracking.
* **Community Group / Social Club:** Focuses on member directories, activity sessions, seasonal subscriptions, and attendance registers.

---

## 2. Branding & White-Labelling

Personalize Admin Centre so your volunteers feel right at home:

1. Go to **Settings &rarr; Organisation Profile**.
2. Upload your **Organisation Logo** (PNG or SVG).
3. Set your **Primary Brand Colour** (used in member portal emails and notifications).
4. Enter your registered charity or company number for automatic inclusion in official receipts and consent forms.

---

## 3. Inviting Your Leadership Team

1. Navigate to **Settings &rarr; Users & Permissions**.
2. Click **+ Invite Team Member**.
3. Enter their email address and select their role:
   * *Admin*, *Safeguarding Officer*, *Rota Coordinator*, or *Staff*.
4. The user receives a secure invite link to create their password and enable two-factor authentication (2FA).
