**Admin Centre** is a secure, cloud-based platform built specifically for UK churches, registered charities, and community non-profits. It brings together member directories, volunteer rotas, safeguarding oversight, and event communication into one intuitive system.

---

## Key Capabilities

* **Adaptive Organisation Modes:** Tailors language and modules to whether you operate as a church congregation, a charitable trust, or a community activity group.
* **Member & Household Directory:** Maintain contact details, family relationships, emergency contacts, and UK GDPR consent records.
* **Smart Volunteer Rotas:** Schedule volunteers across service and event roles with automatic clash detection, availability tracking, and swap requests.
* **Safeguarding & DBS Compliance:** Track Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check numbers, renewal dates, and safeguarding training records.
* **Automated Reminders:** Send email, SMS, and WhatsApp alerts to volunteers before their scheduled dates.

---

## Accessing Admin Centre

Admin Centre is accessible via any modern web browser on desktop, tablet, or mobile at:
`https://admin.alongsidedigital.co.uk` (or your organisation's custom portal link).

### Roles & Security
* **Super Administrators:** Full access to settings, financial records, and all data.
* **Safeguarding Officers:** Dedicated access to DBS dates, training logs, and emergency contact details.
* **Ministry & Team Leaders:** Manage their own volunteer rotas and event attendees.
* **Members & Volunteers:** View their upcoming rota duties and update their personal contact preferences.
