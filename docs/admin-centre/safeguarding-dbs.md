title: Safeguarding & DBS Compliance
description: Manage DBS check dates, renewal notifications, and safeguarding officer permissions.
---

Protecting children, young people, and vulnerable adults is an essential legal and moral duty for any charity or church. Admin Centre gives Safeguarding Officers dedicated tools to manage compliance.

---

## 1. Recording DBS Checks

1. Open the profile of any volunteer working in regulated activity.
2. Select the **Safeguarding** tab.
3. Record their compliance details:
   * **DBS Certificate Number**
   * **Level of Check** (Enhanced, Standard, Barred List check)
   * **Issue Date**
   * **Expiry / 3-Year Renewal Target Date**
   * **DBS Update Service Registration Status**

:::note[Access Restriction]
Safeguarding and DBS data is strictly protected. Only users with the designated **Safeguarding Officer** or **Super Administrator** roles can view certificate details.
:::

---

## 2. Automated Renewal Alerts

Admin Centre monitors renewal dates automatically:
* **90 Days Before Expiry:** The safeguarding officer is flagged on their dashboard.
* **30 Days Before Expiry:** An automated email is sent to the volunteer with instructions on starting their renewal check.
* **Expired Status Warning:** If a volunteer's DBS lapses, a prominent warning indicator appears in the Rota Scheduler if a coordinator attempts to assign them to children's or youth roles.

---

## 3. Training & Certification Logs

Log safeguarding training certificates (e.g. Basic Awareness, Foundation, Leadership Training) alongside proof of completion and course provider details.
