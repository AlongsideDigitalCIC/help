---
title: Volunteer Rotas & Clash Detection
description: Build rotas, prevent scheduling conflicts, and manage volunteer swaps.
---

Scheduling volunteers across multiple ministries or services is one of the most time-consuming jobs in community leadership. Admin Centre automates conflict resolution and communication.

---

## 1. Creating a Rota

1. Navigate to **Rotas** in the main menu.
2. Click **+ New Rota** and assign a name (e.g. *Sunday Service Welcome Team*, *Coffee & Hospitality*, *AV & Sound Desk*).
3. Define the roles required for each date:
   * *e.g., Welcomer (2 needed), Reader (1 needed), Sound Desk Lead (1 needed)*.

---

## 2. Smart Clash Detection

When scheduling volunteers, Admin Centre actively checks for conflicts:

* **Double-Booking Warnings:** If a volunteer is already assigned to the *Children's Ministry* at 10:30 AM, the system warns you if you try to assign them to *Welcome Team* for the same service.
* **Family / Household Sync:** Highlights when two spouses or family members are scheduled at conflicting times.
* **Unavailability & Blackout Dates:** Volunteers can submit vacation and holiday dates directly in their volunteer portal, preventing them from being scheduled when away.

---

## 3. Volunteer Swaps & Replacements

If a volunteer discovers they can no longer attend:
1. They log in to their volunteer portal on mobile or web.
2. Next to their assigned date, they click **Request Swap**.
3. Other qualified team members receive an alert and can volunteer to step in with one click.
4. The rota coordinator is notified automatically once a swap is accepted.
