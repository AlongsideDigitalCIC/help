title: Application Overview
description: A quick visual tour of Presenter's top navigation bar, library sidebar, preview pane, and live broadcast controls.
---

Presenter is organised into **4 clear zones** so volunteers can find media, preview slides, and operate a live service without clutter.

---

## 1. The Top Navigation Bar

Along the top of the window, you switch between your **Service Flow** and your **6 Media Library Tabs**:

| Tab | What It's For |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SERVICE** | Your active running order (**Service Flow**) for today's service or event, with **Clear**, **Reset**, **Load**, and **Save** controls. |
| **SONGS** | Searchable database of your worship songs and hymns (full-text title, artist, and lyric search), plus **Import** and **+ New**. |
| **IMAGES** | Photos, welcome graphics, image-array folders, PDF slide decks, and PowerPoint (`.pptx`) presentations. |
| **BIBLE** | Instant online scripture lookup (via YouVersion) and saved offline Bible passages. |
| **LITURGY** | Call-and-response prayers, creeds, and responsive readings with colour-coded speaker parts. |
| **VIDEOS** | Local video clips as well as direct **YouTube** and **Vimeo** embeds. |
| **MUSIC** | Background and walk-in audio tracks stored in your `Music/` folder. |

In the far top-right corner, the **Settings (gear icon)** opens Display, Overlays, Network (Mobile Remote), CCLI, and General settings.

---

## 2. Left Sidebar (Library / Service Flow)

* When **SERVICE** is selected, the left sidebar shows your **Service Flow**—drag and drop items to reorder them, click any item to cue it in the centre pane, and track which items have already been played.
* When any **Library tab** (**Songs**, **Images**, **Bible**, **Liturgy**, **Videos**, **Music**) is selected, the left sidebar lets you search, browse, create, or import items. Items already added to your current service show a **tick-in-a-circle** badge.

---

## 3. Centre Pane (Preview & Editor)

* Selecting any item in the left sidebar opens all of its slides in the centre pane.
* From here you can:
  * Click **+ Add to Service** to add the item to your running order.
  * For songs, adjust **Lines per Slide (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`)**, open **Flow Arrangement**, click **Edit Song**, or use **Quick Fix** on an individual slide.
  * Click any individual slide tile to send it straight to the projector when live.

---

## 4. Right Panel (Live Controls)

The right-hand panel always stays visible during your gathering:

* **LIVE OUTPUT PREVIEW:** Shows an exact live thumbnail of what the congregation currently sees on the projector.
* **BACKGROUND MUSIC:** Choose a music track or playlist from your `Music/` folder to play quietly before or during the gathering.
* **GO LIVE / END LIVE:** Opens or closes the full-screen audience window on your external projector or TV.
* **BLACK SCREEN:** Instantly blanks the projector screen to black (and click again to return smoothly).
* **SCREEN OVERLAYS (`Alert` & `Timer`):** Display an urgent text alert banner (e.g. a car registration or crèche call) or start a pre-service countdown timer over your screen.
