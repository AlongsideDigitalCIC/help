## 2. Install to Applications

1. Open your **Downloads** folder and double-click the downloaded `.dmg` file.
2. Drag the **Presenter** app icon into your **Applications** folder.
3. Eject the mounted Presenter disk image from Finder.

---

## 3. First-Time Opening (macOS Gatekeeper)

When opening Presenter for the very first time, macOS may ask you to confirm opening an app downloaded from the internet:
1. Open your **Applications** folder.
2. **Right-click** (or `Control + Click`) on **Presenter** and choose **Open**.
3. Click **Open** in the confirmation dialogue. You will only need to do this once.
