---
title: Installing Presenter on Windows
description: How to download and install Presenter on Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit).
---

Presenter runs natively on **Windows 10** and **Windows 11 (64-bit)**.

---

## 1. Download the Installer

1. Visit the official download page at **[alongsidedigital.co.uk/products/presenter/download](https://alongsidedigital.co.uk/products/presenter/download)**.
2. Under **Windows**, choose your preferred installer:
   * **Download `.exe` Installer** *(Recommended for most churches and laptops)*
   * **Download `.msi` Installer** *(Useful for managed networks or IT administrators)*

---

## 2. Run the Installer

1. Open your **Downloads** folder and double-click the downloaded `presenter_..._x64-setup.exe` (or `.msi`) file.
2. Follow the on-screen installation wizard to choose your install location and create a Desktop shortcut.

:::note[Windows SmartScreen Notice]
Windows Defender SmartScreen may display *"Windows protected your PC"* when running a newly downloaded installer for the first time. Click **More info** and then **Run anyway** to proceed with the installation.
:::

---

## 3. Launch & Connect Your Media Folder

1. Open **Presenter** from your Start Menu or Desktop shortcut.
2. On first launch, Presenter will prompt you to select a local folder for your media library. Follow **[Connecting Your Media Folder](/presenter/media-folder-setup)** to complete setup.
