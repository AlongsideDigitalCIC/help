Presenter's **Images** library holds standalone graphics, multi-slide image folders, **PDF** slide decks, and **PowerPoint (`.pptx`)** presentations.

---

## 1. Supported Formats

* **Images:** `.png`, `.jpg` / `.jpeg`, `.webp`, `.gif`
* **Slide Decks:** `.pdf` and `.pptx` (PowerPoint)
* **Image Folders:** Place a folder containing numbered images (e.g. `slide-1.png`, `slide-2.png`) inside your `Images/` folder and Presenter treats the whole folder as an ordered slide deck!

---

## 2. How to Add Files to Your Images Library

1. Open your connected **`Presenter\Images\`** folder in File Explorer (Windows) or Finder (macOS).
2. Copy or move your graphics, `.pdf` files, `.pptx` files, or image subfolders into **`Presenter\Images\`**.
3. Switch to the **Images** tab in Presenter—your new graphics and slide decks appear automatically in the left-hand sidebar.

:::tip[Guest Speaker PowerPoint Slides]
Presenter natively converts `.pptx` and `.pdf` files into high-resolution slides. If a guest speaker uses unusual custom fonts inside PowerPoint, ask them to export their slides as a **PDF** so every font and diagram renders identically on your church computer.
:::
