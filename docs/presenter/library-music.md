---
title: Adding Background Music to Your Library
description: Add MP3, WAV, M4A, AAC, OGG, or FLAC audio tracks to Presenter's Music library for walk-in and prayer playlists.
---

Presenter includes a built-in background music player so you can play gentle walk-in music before a service, reflective tracks during prayer or communion, or audio clips during an event.

---

## 1. Supported Audio Formats

* `.mp3`
* `.wav`
* `.m4a` / `.aac`
* `.ogg`
* `.flac`

---

## 2. Adding Music Tracks to Your Library

1. Open your connected **`Presenter\Music\`** folder on your computer.
2. Copy your audio files (or organise them into subfolders inside `Music/`) into **`Presenter\Music\`**.
3. Open Presenter and click the **Music** tab along the top bar—all tracks appear ready to preview or select in the **Background Music** dropdown in the right-hand Live Controls panel.

:::note[Music Licensing Reminder]
If you play recorded background music in public gatherings or churches in the UK, ensure your organisation holds the appropriate music licence (such as a PPL Church Licence / CCLI Music Reproduction coverage where applicable).
:::
