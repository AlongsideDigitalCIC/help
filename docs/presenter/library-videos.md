---
title: Adding Videos to Your Library (Local, YouTube & Vimeo)
description: Store local video clips in your Presenter library or load YouTube and Vimeo videos directly into Presenter.
---

Presenter plays both **local video files** (stored offline on your computer) and **online YouTube / Vimeo links** without needing a separate media player or web browser window.

---

## 1. Adding Local Video Files (Recommended for Offline Reliability)

1. Open your connected **`Presenter\Videos\`** folder on your computer.
2. Copy your `.mp4`, `.webm`, or `.mov` video files into **`Presenter\Videos\`**.
3. In Presenter, click the **Videos** tab along the top bar—your local videos appear immediately in the left-hand sidebar.

---

## 2. Using YouTube & Vimeo Links

When your church has a reliable internet connection, you can also load online videos straight from the **Videos** tab:

1. Click the **Videos** tab in Presenter.
2. Paste a **YouTube** (`youtube.com/watch?v=...` or `youtu.be/...`) or **Vimeo** (`vimeo.com/...`) URL into the external video box.
3. Presenter automatically fetches the video title for you (you can also type a custom title).
4. Click to preview the video, then click **+ Add to Service** to include it in your Service Flow.

:::caution[Reliability Tip]
Online videos depend on your venue's internet connection. Whenever you have permission to download a video (such as a charity appeal video or baptism reflection), saving it as an `.mp4` inside `Presenter\Videos\` guarantees smooth, buffer-free playback even if church Wi-Fi drops.
:::
