---
title: Going Live, Black Screen & Shortcuts
description: How to launch the live output window, advance slides, use Black Screen blanking, and operate smoothly during a service.
---

Once your **Service Flow** is ready and your projector is configured in Extended Display mode (see **[Dual-Screen & Projector Setup](/presenter/presentation-mode)**), you are ready to run your live gathering.

---

## 1. Starting & Ending Live Output

1. Click the **GO LIVE** button in the right-hand Live Controls panel.
2. Presenter opens a clean, borderless full-screen output window on your configured projector display, and the **LIVE OUTPUT PREVIEW** thumbnail at the top right shows exactly what your congregation sees.
3. Click any item in your **Service Flow**, then click a slide tile in the centre pane to project it live.
4. When your gathering finishes, click **END LIVE** to close the external audience window.

---

## 2. Black Screen (Instant Blanking)

During a sermon, prayer, or transition when you want nothing projected on screen:
* Click the **BLACK SCREEN** button beneath **GO LIVE** (or press the **`B`** key).
* The projector smoothly fades to a clean black screen while keeping your place ready in the Service Flow.
* Click **BLACK SCREEN** (or press **`B`**) again to bring the current slide right back up.

---

## 3. Useful Live Keyboard Shortcuts

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Space` or `Right Arrow` | Advance to the next slide |
| `Left Arrow` | Return to the previous slide |
| `B` | Toggle **Black Screen** |
| `C` | **Clear Text** (hides lyrics/text while keeping the background) |
| `Esc` | Close the live audience window |

---

## 4. Live Video & Audio Scrubbing

When projecting a local video or audio clip, playback controls (Play/Pause, progress scrub bar, and mute/volume) appear directly below the **LIVE OUTPUT PREVIEW** panel so you can control playback without leaving Presenter.
