---
title: Reordering, Saving & Loading Services
description: Organise your running order with drag-and-drop, save services ahead of time, load saved plans, and reset played statuses.
---

Once you have added your songs, readings, liturgies, videos, and slides, switch to the **SERVICE** tab on the top bar to manage your complete running order.

---

## 1. Reordering Your Service Flow

* Click and hold the **grip handle (`⋮⋮`)** on the left side of any item in the **Service Flow** panel.
* Drag the item up or down to its new position and release.

---

## 2. Service Flow Toolbar Controls

At the top of the **Service Flow** panel you have four quick action buttons:

| Button | What It Does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **SAVE** | Saves your current running order to a service file so you can prepare midweek and open it on Sunday morning. |
| **LOAD** | Opens a previously saved service file into your Service Flow. |
| **RESET** | Clears all **"Played" checkmarks** across your service items (useful after a rehearsal or between a 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM service). |
| **CLEAR** | Removes all items from the current Service Flow so you can start building a new service from scratch. |

---

## 3. Removing a Single Item

Hover over any item in the **Service Flow** and click the **trash icon** to remove it from today's service (this only removes it from the Service Flow—it never deletes the song or file from your Library).
