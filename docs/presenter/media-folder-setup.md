description: Set up your local Presenter Media Library folder on first launch so your songs, slides, videos, and music stay organised and offline-ready.

Presenter stores all of your songs, images, Bibles, liturgies, videos, and background music directly in a normal folder on your computer—meaning you always own your files, nothing is locked in a proprietary cloud, and everything works completely offline.

---

## 1. Selecting Your Library Folder on First Launch

When you launch Presenter for the very first time, you will see the **Media Library Setup** screen:

1. Click **Select Library Folder** (recommended location: your **Documents** folder, or a synced folder such as **OneDrive**, **Google Drive**, or **Dropbox** if multiple volunteers prepare services across computers).
2. Choose the parent folder (for example `Documents`) and click **Select Folder**.
3. Presenter will automatically create a **`Presenter`** folder inside it with 6 organised subfolders:
   * `Songs/` — Your worship songs saved as clean `.md` Markdown files
   * `Images/` — Slide decks (`.pdf`, `.pptx`), image folders, and standalone graphics
   * `Videos/` — Local `.mp4`, `.webm`, and `.mov` video files
   * `Music/` — Background and walk-in `.mp3`, `.wav`, and `.m4a` audio tracks
   * `Bible/` — Cached offline Bible passages and local JSON translations
   * `Liturgy/` — Call-and-response readings and prayers saved as `.md` files

:::tip[Syncing Across Church Computers]
If your service leader prepares slides at home and your AV volunteer runs them on Sunday at church, place your `Presenter` library folder inside a shared **OneDrive**, **Google Drive**, or **Dropbox** folder. Any new songs or saved services sync automatically!
:::

---

## 2. Changing Your Media Folder Later

You can switch to a different library folder at any time:
1. Click the **Settings** (gear icon) in the top-right corner of Presenter.
2. Open the **General** tab.
3. Under **Storage**, click **Change Library Folder** and select your new folder.
