Presenter allows you to integrate multimedia elements directly into your service schedule without opening separate third-party media players.

---

## 1. Supported Media Formats

* **Video:** MP4, WebM (up to 4K resolution).
* **Audio:** MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A, OGG.
* **Images:** PNG, JPEG, WebP, SVG.
* **Presentations:** PDF slide decks.

---

## 2. Playing Videos Smoothly

1. In the Service Order, click **+ Add Item &rarr; Video**.
2. Select a local video file from your computer.
3. Configure playback options:
   * **Auto-Play:** Begins playing immediately upon selecting the slide.
   * **Audio Routing:** Output sound to your system default sound card (e.g. HDMI or sound desk USB audio interface).
   * **Loop:** Useful for pre-service welcome loops or countdown clocks.
4. During playback, the operator view shows elapsed time, remaining time, and a scrub bar.

---

## 4. PDF Presentations

Guest speakers often bring slides in PDF or PowerPoint format:
1. Save or export their presentation as a **PDF**.
2. Drag and drop the PDF into the Service Order.
3. Presenter renders each page as a high-resolution slide that can be navigated with your remote or spacebar.
