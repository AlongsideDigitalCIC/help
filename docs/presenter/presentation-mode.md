title: Display & Dual-Screen Setup
description: How to configure Windows, macOS, and Presenter for multi-display projection.
---

Presenter is designed to give your operator full control on their laptop screen while sending a clean, borderless output to your projector or congregation display.

---

## 1. Setting Up Your Computer's Display Mode

Before opening Presenter's projection window, verify your operating system display settings:

### On Windows 10 & 11
1. Connect your projector/TV to your computer.
2. Press `Windows Key + P` on your keyboard.
3. Select **Extend** (Do not use *Duplicate* or *PC screen only*).
4. Right-click your desktop and choose **Display settings** to check which monitor is `1` and `2`.

### On macOS
1. Connect your external screen.
2. Open **System Settings &rarr; Displays**.
3. Ensure your external screen is set to **Extended Display** (not Mirror).

---

## 2. Opening the Audience Window in Presenter

1. In the top-right toolbar of Presenter, click the **Display / Projector** icon.
2. Select the display corresponding to your projector (usually labelled *Display 2*).
3. Click **Go Live**.
4. A full-screen, frameless window will appear on the projector displaying your active slide.

---

## 3. Keyboard Shortcuts for Live Operation

| Shortcut | Action |
| :--- | :--- |
| `Space` or `Right Arrow` | Advance to the next slide |
| `Left Arrow` | Return to the previous slide |
| `B` | **Blackout:** Turns the projector display completely black |
| `C` | **Clear Text:** Hides the lyrics/scripture while retaining the background |
| `L` | **Logo Screen:** Displays your church/organisation logo graphic |
| `Esc` | Close the live projector output window |
