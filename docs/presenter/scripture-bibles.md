---
title: Scripture & Bible Versions
description: Look up, format, and display scripture readings during services.
---

Presenter makes displaying scripture effortless, supporting both online lookups (via YouVersion) and offline local Bibles for environments without reliable internet.

---

## 1. Quick Scripture Lookup

1. Click the **Bible** tab in the top navigation bar.
2. Select your desired translation from the dropdown list (online via YouVersion or any local offline translation stored in `Presenter\Bible\`).
3. In the **Bible Quick-Search** box, type your reference (book autocomplete suggestions appear as you type) and click **FIND** or press `Enter`:
   * *Examples:* `John 3:16`, `Psalm 23`, `Romans 8:28-39`
   * Alternatively, click **Browse Books** to pick any Old or New Testament book and chapter.
4. Review the formatted verse slides in the centre preview panel, and adjust **Verses** per slide (`1`–`4`) in the preview toolbar if needed.
5. Click **Add Chapter to Service** (or select specific slides and click **Add Verses**) to add the passage to your **Service Flow**.

---

## 2. Supported Translations & Offline Bibles

### Online Lookup (YouVersion)
When connected to the internet, Presenter fetches passages directly across English translations available via YouVersion (such as NIV, KJV, ESV, NLT, ASV, and more).

### Local Offline Bibles & Saved Verses
For venues without reliable Wi-Fi:
1. Place local Bible book JSON files inside a translation subfolder within your connected Media Library folder (for example `Presenter\Bible\KJV\`). Presenter scans this folder automatically and lists it under **Local Bibles (Offline)** in the translation dropdown.
2. You can also access previously saved passages under **Saved Offline Verses** in the **Bible** sidebar so key readings remain ready even without an internet connection.

---

## 3. Formatting Verse Slides

* **Automatic Verse Numbering:** Presenter displays verse numbers cleanly alongside the scripture text.
* **Adjustable Verses per Slide:** Use the **Verses** selector (`1`, `2`, `3`, `4`) in the centre preview toolbar to balance legibility and slide count.
* **Automatic Passage Reference:** Each slide shows the exact book, chapter, and verse range currently on screen.
