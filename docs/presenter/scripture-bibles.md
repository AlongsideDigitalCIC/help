Presenter makes displaying scripture effortless, supporting both online lookups (via YouVersion) and offline local Bibles for environments without reliable internet.

---

## 1. Quick Scripture Lookup

1. In the Service Order pane, click **+ Add Item &rarr; Scripture**.
2. In the search box, enter your reference:
   * *Examples:* `John 3:16`, `Psalm 23`, `Romans 8:28-39`
3. Select your desired translation from the dropdown list.
4. Review the auto-split verses in the preview panel.
5. Click **Insert into Service Order**.

---

## 2. Supported Translations & Offline Bibles

### Online Lookup
When connected to the internet, Presenter can fetch passages directly across popular public translations.

### Offline JSON Bibles
For venues without reliable Wi-Fi:
1. Open **Settings &rarr; Bibles**.
2. Click **Download Offline Translations**.
3. Choose your preferred public domain or licensed translations.
4. Once downloaded, these remain available permanently on the local machine with zero internet required.
