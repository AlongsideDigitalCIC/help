---
title: Adding Bible Readings to Your Service
description: Look up a scripture passage or select a cached offline reading and add it to your Presenter Service Flow.
---

You can add Bible readings to your service ahead of time—or pull up an unexpected scripture reference live during a sermon in seconds.

---

## Step-by-Step

1. Click the **Bible** tab along the top navigation bar.
2. Choose your Bible translation from the dropdown list (online via YouVersion or any local offline translation in `Presenter\Bible\`).
3. Type the passage reference into the search box (for example `Psalm 23`, `John 3:16-21`, or `Philippians 4:4-8`)—or browse by Book and Chapter—and press **Enter**.
4. Review the auto-formatted verse slides in the centre preview pane.
5. Click **+ Add to Service** to place the passage in your **Service Flow**.

:::note[Offline Caching]
Passages you look up can also be saved in your **Saved Offline Verses** list in the left-hand sidebar so they remain accessible even if your venue's internet disconnects on Sunday morning.
:::
