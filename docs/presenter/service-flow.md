---
title: Adding Songs & Building Your Service Flow
description: Step-by-step guide for finding songs in your library, adding them to your Service Flow, and managing your order of service in Presenter.
---

Your **Service Flow** is the running order of songs, readings, liturgies, videos, and slides for your gathering or church service. Follow these steps to add songs from your library into your Service Flow.

---

## 1. Adding a Song to Your Service

### Step 1: Select Songs
To add a song to your service list, first click on the **Songs** tab on the Library navigation bar along the top of the Presenter application.

---

### Step 2: Search Your Library
All of your songs appear in the left-hand **Songs Library** navigation panel. You can scroll through your list to find the song you want, or type into the **Search Library...** box to search by **song title**, **artist**, or **lyrics** to locate it quickly.

---

### Step 3: Select the Song
Once you have found the song you want, click on the **song tile** in the left-hand navigation panel to open its preview.

---

### Step 4: Preview & Click Add to Service
Review the song slides in the preview pane to make sure it is the right version, then click **Add to Service** to add the song to your service order.

---

### Step 5: Confirmation
Once added, the song tile in the left-hand navigation panel will display a **tick inside a circle** icon next to the song name, confirming it is in your current service.

---

### Step 6: Check Your Service Flow
Switch back to the **Service** tab on the top navigation bar. You will see your newly added song listed in your **Service Flow**, ready to display when you go live.

---

## 2. Managing Your Service Flow

Once you have added songs and media to your **Service Flow**, you can organise and save your order using the controls at the top of the left-hand **Service Flow** panel:

* **Reorder Items:** Click and drag any item up or down using the grip handle on the left of its tile.
* **Save:** Saves your complete running order to a file so you can prepare a service ahead of time or archive it.
* **Load:** Opens a previously saved service order file.
* **Reset:** Clears the played checkmarks across your service items (handy if you are running a rehearsal or repeating the same service twice in one day).
* **Clear:** Empties the current Service Flow so you can start fresh for a new gathering.
