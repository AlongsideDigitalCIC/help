---
title: Adding Images & Slide Decks to Your Service
description: Add single graphics, announcement loops, PDF slide decks, and PowerPoint presentations to your Presenter Service Flow.
---

Once your images, `.pdf`, or `.pptx` files are stored in `Presenter\Images\`, adding them to your service takes just two clicks.

---

## Step-by-Step

1. Click the **Images** tab along the top navigation bar.
2. Click any image, image folder, `.pdf` file, or `.pptx` presentation in the left-hand sidebar.
3. Review the rendered slides in the centre preview pane.
4. Click **+ Add to Service** at the top of the preview pane.
5. A tick-in-a-circle badge confirms the item is in your service. Switch to the **Service** tab to drag it into the right position in your **Service Flow**.

:::tip[Pre-Service Announcement Loops]
If you put a folder of announcement images (or a multi-page notices PDF) into your Service Flow, you can use Presenter's **Auto Timer** when live to cycle through announcement slides automatically before the gathering starts.
:::
