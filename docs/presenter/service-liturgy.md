---
title: Adding Liturgy to Your Service
description: Add saved prayers, creeds, and responsive readings from your Liturgy Library into your Service Flow.
---

Adding a prepared creed, confession, or responsive prayer to your service takes just a moment:

---

## Step-by-Step

1. Click the **Liturgy** tab on the top navigation bar.
2. In the left-hand **Liturgy Library**, click the liturgy file you want to use (for example *The Lord's Prayer* or *Call to Worship*).
3. Inspect the formatted speaker and congregation response slides in the centre preview pane.
4. Click **+ Add to Service**.
5. Switch back to the **Service** tab to position your liturgy at the right moment in your **Service Flow**.
