---
title: Adding Background Music to Your Service
description: Play walk-in background music playlists from the right-hand Live Controls or add standalone audio tracks to your Service Flow.
---

Presenter gives you **two ways** to use audio tracks from your `Presenter\Music\` folder during a gathering:

---

## Option 1: Background Music Player (Walk-In & Prayer Music)

The **Background Music** control sits permanently in the right-hand Live Controls panel so music can keep playing smoothly while you show welcome slides or notices:

1. In the right-hand panel under **BACKGROUND MUSIC**, open the dropdown and select a track or folder from your `Music/` library.
2. Click **Play** to start the audio.
3. Use the playback mode toggle to switch between **Playlist Repeat**, **Single Track Repeat**, or **Play Once**.
4. Adjust volume or click **Pause / Stop** when the service leader steps up to begin.

---

## Option 2: Adding an Audio Track Directly to Your Service Flow

If a specific audio clip (such as a recorded backing track or special item) needs to happen at a set point in your service order:

1. Click the **Music** tab along the top navigation bar.
2. Select the audio file from the left-hand sidebar.
3. Click **+ Add to Service** to place it in your **Service Flow**.
