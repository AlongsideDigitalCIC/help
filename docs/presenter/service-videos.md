---
title: Adding Videos to Your Service
description: Add local video files or YouTube/Vimeo embeds to your Service Flow and configure playback options.
---

Videos sit right alongside songs and scripture inside your **Service Flow**, so you never need to alt-tab out to VLC or a browser window during a service.

---

## Step-by-Step

1. Click the **Videos** tab on the top navigation bar.
2. Either:
   * Click a **local video file** (`.mp4`, `.webm`, `.mov`) from the left-hand sidebar, **or**
   * Paste a **YouTube** or **Vimeo** URL into the external video box and load it.
3. Preview the video in the centre pane and click **+ Add to Service**.
4. Switch to the **Service** tab to position the video in your **Service Flow**.

---

## Auto-Play Option

In your **Service Flow**, video items include an **Auto-Play** toggle so you can choose whether the video begins playing immediately when selected live or waits until you click **Play** in the live controls panel.
