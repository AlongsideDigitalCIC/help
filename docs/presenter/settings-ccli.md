description: Configure your church CCLI Licence Number, on-screen lyric attribution placement, and export annual CCLI song history CSV reports.
---

Presenter makes staying compliant with your **CCLI Church Copyright Licence** effortless, both on screen during worship and when submitting your annual song usage report.

---

## 1. Entering Your Church CCLI Licence Number

1. Click the **Settings (gear icon)** in the top-right corner of Presenter.
2. Select the **Song History & CCLI** tab.
3. Enter your **Church CCLI License Number** (for example `1234567`).

---

## 2. Customising On-Screen Attribution

Under **Lyric Attribution Display Mode**, choose when copyright and licence credits appear during songs:

* **First Slide Only** *(Recommended)* — Shows title, writers, copyright, CCLI Song #, and your Church Licence # on Slide 1, keeping the rest of your worship slides clean and distraction-free.
* **Last Slide Only** — Shows legal attribution on the final slide of each song.
* **First & Last Slides** — Displays credits on both the opening and closing slide.
* **All Slides** — Displays a subtle attribution line on every slide.
* **Off / Hidden** — Hides on-screen attribution (only recommended if copyright details are printed in your service sheet/bulletin).

You can also choose **Screen Footer Alignment** (**Bottom Left**, **Bottom Center**, or **Bottom Right**) and test how it looks immediately using the **Live Screen Simulation Preview** on the right.

---

## 3. Exporting Your Annual CCLI Song History Report

Presenter automatically logs every song projected live during your services. To export your report:

1. Open **Settings &rarr; Song History & CCLI** and scroll down to **Song History & Annual CCLI Reporting**.
2. Choose your **Reporting Date From** and **Reporting Date To**.
3. Click **Export Song History to CSV**.

The downloaded `.csv` file includes **Date Played**, **Artist**, **Song Title**, **CCLI Song Number**, and **Copyright details**, ready to open in Excel or submit to CCLI Reporting.
