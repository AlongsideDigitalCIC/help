---
title: Display, Fonts & Backgrounds Settings
description: Configure target projector display, typography, text scaling, slide transitions, and category backgrounds in Presenter Settings.
---

Click the **Settings (gear icon)** in the top-right corner of Presenter and open the **Display & Transitions** tab to customise how slides look on screen.

---

## 1. Projector Monitor Output

* Choose which physical screen Presenter opens its full-screen window on when you click **GO LIVE** (or leave on **Auto** to target your external projector/TV automatically).
* Presenter remembers your monitor selection automatically between services.

---

## 2. Typography & Text Scale

* **Display Font:** Choose from 10 built-in, offline-ready presentation fonts (*Inter*, *Roboto*, *Open Sans*, *Montserrat*, *Lato*, *Merriweather*, *Lora*, *Playfair Display*, *Crimson Pro*, and *EB Garamond*).
* **Output Text Scale:** Fine-tune global text sizing so lyrics and scripture remain easy to read from the very back row of your venue.

---

## 3. Category Backgrounds & Slide Transitions

Presenter lets you set separate default backgrounds for each content type so your worship, scripture, and liturgy screens each have a cohesive visual identity:

* **Songs**
* **Bible**
* **Liturgy**

For each category, choose a **Solid Colour**, a **Static Image**, or a subtle **Motion Video**, and adjust the **Background Dim** slider so white lyrics always maintain strong contrast and legibility. You can also choose a **Slide Transition** (*Crossfade*, *Fade Through*, *Slide Left*, or *Gentle Rise*) and preview it live inside Settings. *(Countdown timer backdrops are configured in the **Announcements & Timer** tab.)*
