We want to ensure your technology runs smoothly so you can focus on your people and community.

### Get in Touch

If you have questions, run into a bug, or need advice on setting up your systems:

* **Email:** [help@alongsidedigital.co.uk](mailto:help@alongsidedigital.co.uk)
* **Response Times:** We monitor emails closely throughout the week, with dedicated volunteer-support focus over weekends.

---

### Tips for Faster Support

When emailing us about an issue, please include:

1. **Which product** you are using (**Presenter** or **Admin Centre**).
2. **Device / Operating System** (e.g. Windows 11, macOS, Chrome on iPad).
3. **What happened** and what you were trying to do.
4. **Screenshots or photos** of your screen if there is an error message.

---

### Feature Requests & Community Feedback

Alongside Digital CIC is a community-first organisation. If you have an idea for how Presenter or Admin Centre can better serve your church or charity, let us know at [help@alongsidedigital.co.uk](mailto:help@alongsidedigital.co.uk).
